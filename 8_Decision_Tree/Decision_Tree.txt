2. Entropy and Information Gain
1️⃣ Entropy – measures the "impurity" (or disorder) of a group:
  - If all the items belong to one class → Entropy = 0
  - If the group is half class A and half class B → Entropy = 1 (max disorder)
  - Formula: Entropy(S)= ∑pi.log2(pi)

2️⃣ Information Gain – how much entropy decreases after a split:
  - It tells you how good a feature is at separating the classes.
  - Formula: Information Gain= Entropy(𝑆)−∑(∣𝑆𝑗∣/∣𝑆∣)⋅Entropy(𝑆𝑗)
    Where:
      - 𝑆 is the current dataset
      - 𝑆𝑗 is the subset after splitting on a feature
      - ∣𝑆𝑗∣/∣𝑆∣ is the weight (how big the subset is compared to the whole)
